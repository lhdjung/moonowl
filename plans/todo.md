## Concrete issues

### Comparison

- **A footnote at the foot of a page or column stands in the middle of the
  text that runs on after it.** A word broken there is joined to the
  footnote's first word ("self-" and "1" in a journal's layout), and the
  footnote is reported as a passage that moved. Telling a footnote from the
  text needs its size and its place on the page.
- **What a journal adds to the first page is reported as added**: its
  name, the copyright line, the DOI and the dates. It is added text, but
  none of it is the authors'.
